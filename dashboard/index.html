Valuation Model Methodology & Category Multiples

The implied valuation model uses a multi-factor formula built entirely from the sealed DefiLlama snapshot:

Implied Value = TVL × Category Multiple × Growth Multiplier × Maturity Multiplier × Chain Multiplier

1. Category Multiples (Live Comps)

Computed dynamically from the median market cap to TVL ratio of protocols with active tokens in each category within the snapshot. Clamped between 0.08x and 2.8x.

2. Growth Momentum

7-day TVL change percentage clamped between -50% and +100%, scaled by weight 0.4.

3. Maturity & Track Record

Age in days from `listed_at` to `as_of`, scaled from 0.7 to 1.0 (saturating at 3 years). Rewards enduring protocols without tokens.

4. Chain Breadth

1 + 0.04 × min(chains.count, 5), rewarding multi-chain deployment reach.
